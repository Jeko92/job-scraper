# JobScraper

## Vision

Job sourcing is accessible for everyone, automated with agentic tools, and separate from recruiters influence. 

## Mission

Help Job Seekers to find suitable jobs on the market by using modern AI tools, aggregate findings to benefit all Job Seekers, and provide workflows for better decision making. 

## Stage 1 (Profile Creation)

* The Job Seeker chooses from a collection of industries and skills in what kind of jobs the Portal should investigate.
* The Job Seeker can take a questionnaire if they are in a phase of redirection. 
* The Job Seeker creates a persistent profile. 

## Stage 2 (Hunt and Gather)

* Based on the Job Seeker interest and skills, a model searched for companies in that field. 
* Based on the list of companies, it expands its search to neighboring companies (competitors). 
* Based on the the expanded list of companies, a model researched each company website and collects information in a structured manner. 
* Timestamp findings to invalidate old data. 
* Findings are shared Portal-wide while the Job Seeker gets information about their field of interest.

## Stage 3 (Runners)

* Run periodically in the background to match Job Seekers with positions based on job descriptions. 
* Push notify Job Seekers. 
* Propose a cover letter fitting to the Job Seeker profile and job description. Propose an email text. 
* Provide an email address or link to the form.
* Provide information about the company, as in number of employees, reputation, competition, company structure, 
* Human in the loop

## Stage 4 (Advisors)

* Keep an overview of the scraped and applied jobs. 
* Collect information about the jobs and applications for future data driven decision making.
* Periodically get feedback about the progress tailored to the combination of Job Seeker, industry, and past actions. 
* Market research. Plot job trends. Recommend new skills to learn. 

## Extras

* Apply automatically
* Personal writing style
