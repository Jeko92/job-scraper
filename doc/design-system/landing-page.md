# Landing page

This file documents the thought process for the landingpage design. 

## Shapes

Currently, Apple and other design-leading companies stayed with a grid of cards, but they shifted to hard edges. Rounded corners are gone. 

We will stay with the corners and cards that we already have. Including at the landingpage. 

## Colors

We definitely stick with the color palate that has been decided on already. It's plausible to be more creative and specifically use the accent color more prominently. 

## Gradients

Gradients are welcomed. Neuemorphism is encouraged when used sparingly. 

## Blur

Despite it's popularity, there is no blur currently in the interface, nor in the design-system specified. I recommend to keep it that way.

## Animations

Animations are welcomed on the landingpage.

### Hero animation

For the hero animation, we use the shapes and preliminary animation we already have.

The animation resembles the process of: 
1. Getting together of company and candidate. 
2. Conversation, getting to know each other, and negotiation
3. Match

The following steps loop indefinitely: 
1. Get-together: Circle and dot are coming in from the sides. One is a bit higher, the other a bit lower. 
2. Converse: Circle and dot are spinning each other. 
3. Match: Circle and dot finally overlap and bounce once as if they are clicked like a button. 
4. Clean-up stage: Circle and dot are moving towards the screen/off the surface and fade out. 

## Pictures

We don't plan to have pictures of people on the landingpage since our primary design-language is neutral and genderless, which would be thrown over by skin and faces. 

## White space

We stay with the white space that we already have. 

## Structure

1. Hero
2. Problem statement
3. Solution
4. Perks
5. Building trust
6. Special offer/pricing

### Hero

The hero describes in maximum three words, what the website offers. Example: "Find hiring companies".

If it's not clear what the company/website offers within the first two seconds, it's a miss. 

### Problem statement

* Portals are overcrowded
* Companies create ghost postings (less so on their website)
* The market is saturated

### Solution

* Talking to companies directly
* Significant competitive edge due to far less competition

### Perks

Getting to a new job using modern technology and having access to a team of experts and community. 

The company positions itself as a single focus service provider and therefore is optimized for the problem statement and brings expertise.

### Bilding trust

Show some picture or graphic that finally builds trust in the product or company. Something that makes it appear IRL. 

If necessary, a carousel of Trustpilot testimonials with only small pictures. 

### Special offer/pricing

Three pricing stages: 
1. The first one has not nearly any use.
2. The actual price you want people to click on.
3. Way above useful to make the previous price look cheap. 

All prices appear as discounts.

### Extra: Newsletter

Just to make clear that the page ends, and to not cut off right after prices, present an obnoxiously big newsletter sign-up.
