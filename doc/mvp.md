# MVP

"A minimum viable product is a version of a product with just enough features to be usable by early customers who can then provide feedback for future product development. A focus on releasing an MVP means that developers potentially avoid lengthy and unnecessary work."

The MVP includes the following functionality:

## Landing-page

A landing-page is a static resource (static website) that promotes the Platform and funnels visitor-action by CTAs. It has mainly marketing purposes. 

## Profiles

Job Seekers can create their user profile for the platform with classic username/email and password combinations. We omit password-less or MFA (Multi-factor authentication) options for now. Forgetting the password and email-verification are also not included in the MVP.

## Login and Authentication

Job Seekers can login with their credentials (username/email and password). Routes that afford authentication are secured with methods that were taught in the Bootcamp. Job Seekers have the option to edit their profiles. 

## Gathering data from the user

The interface prompts the Job Seeker for a minimal information after the first successful login, before the first use of the Platform. The information should entail the very necessary information for minimal functionality. The profile grows in detail as the Job Seeker uses the Platform. 

## Crawl company websites

The MVP should show at least one successful case of crawling job offers from company websites. Meta-information about the company and inferred information about the job position are stored persistently. 

## Display jobs

The interface displays suitable jobs to a Job Seeker. The MVP also verifies that the Job Seeker is only served with suitable jobs. Each listed job can be clicked on to reveal details about the position and company.
